SECTION 6: Publication gap assessment summary
=================================================================

Existing published studies (from literature search):
  1. Kimani et al 2014 (IJEOH): Women 15-49, 2008-09 KDHS IR — determinants
  2. Kazungu & Barasa 2017 (TMIH): Women 15-49, 2014 KDHS IR — levels, correlates
  3. Barasa et al 2021 (BMJ GH): 36 SSA countries, all pop, single cross-section
  4. Medrxiv 2025 (Aldousari): All Kenyans 2022 KDHS — media/digital access only
  5. FSD Kenya 2025: National household survey 2024 — not DHS-based

GAPS not covered by any published study:
  - Multi-wave (2008, 2014, 2022) TREND analysis for ALL population groups
  - Children under 5, adolescents 5-17, older adults 60+ — NEVER analyzed
  - Sex-stratified trends (men vs women at all ages)
  - Disability x insurance interaction across time
  - Concentration index TREND (inequality trajectory) for all ages
  - Vulnerable group profiling: children, elderly, disabled, rural poor
  - All of this using PR files from 3 waves — population-representative

PROPOSED NEW STUDY (ST06 revised or new ST09):
  Title candidate:
  'Who is left behind? Health insurance coverage, equity trends, and
   vulnerable group profiles across the Kenyan population 2008-2022:
   A multi-wave analysis of KDHS household survey data'

  Key additions over ST06:
   * Full population, not just women 15-49
   * Sex disaggregation
   * Life-course age groups (children, adolescents, working-age, elderly)
   * Disability interaction
   * HH-head status (are heads better covered than other members?)
   * Insurance type trends across population sub-groups
   * Policy relevance: directly informs SHA targeting for exemptions
